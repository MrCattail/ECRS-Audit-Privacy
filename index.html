ECRS Price Audit Privacy Policy

Effective August 20, 2026

ECRS Price Audit is an internal inventory-audit tool for authorized Metro Chef staff. It does not sell user data or use it for advertising.

Purpose

ECRS Price Audit supports Metro Chef's internal inventory-audit process. Its single purpose is to record authorized ECRS inventory item-page visits, observed Base Price changes, and verification results, and to display the audit history for each item.

Information handled

The extension processes the signed-in ECRS account display name, item PK, Item ID, item name, Base Price values, Last Modified value, current ECRS item-page address, timestamps, computer name, extension version, event identifiers, and verification status.

A per-computer authentication token is stored in Chrome extension storage and sent over HTTPS to authenticate requests to the audit service. It is not included in audit-event records.

The extension does not read or store ECRS passwords, browser cookies, unrelated browsing activity, personal financial or payment information, health information, personal communications, precise location, or complete page HTML.

Use and sharing

Audit data is used only to provide, maintain, and secure the internal ECRS audit feature. Records are sent over HTTPS to Metro Chef's secured audit service, which is hosted by Supabase as a service provider. The service and hosting provider may necessarily process standard connection metadata, such as an IP address, to deliver and secure requests.

Metro Chef does not sell audit data, use it for advertising, use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending, or transfer it for purposes unrelated to the extension's single purpose. ECRS Price Audit's use and transfer of information complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Local storage and security

The extension stores its configuration, disclosure consent, per-computer authentication token, pending audit records, permanently failed records, queue-health statistics, and pending price confirmations in Chrome extension storage. Unsynchronized records remain locally until upload succeeds. Permanently rejected records remain locally until an authorized user retries them or exports and confirms their removal.

All audit-service communications use HTTPS. The extension does not download or execute remotely hosted code.

Choice and control

Auditing remains disabled until the user accepts the extension's data disclosure and device setup is complete. Authorized users can inspect queue health, retry or export rejected records, and clear only the exact rejected-record revision that was exported.

Retention and requests

Server retention, access, correction, and deletion are controlled by Metro Chef's approved internal audit policy and authorized administrators.

For privacy questions or requests, email customer.service@metrochef.com or use the Metro Chef contact page. Do not send passwords, device tokens, or other credentials in a support request.

Published for the ECRS Price Audit Chrome extension.
